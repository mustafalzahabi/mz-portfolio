Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

mz-portfolio is a free, open-source website that turns your information into a portfolio. It runs entirely in your browser. It has no database and does not store your data on its own servers.

Where your portfolio is stored

  • Signed in with GitHub: your portfolio is a resume.json gist on your GitHub account, and blog posts are gists you own.
  • Signed in with Google: your portfolio is a folder named "mz-portfolio" in your Google Drive, holding resume.json, your posts and images you upload.
  • Signed in with both, linked into one account: the link (your GitHub username, your Google email and your Drive folder's id, plus your Account settings choices) is saved privately in both: a secret gist and the app's hidden folder in your Drive. The published portfolio lists only its two public addresses, never your email, so it can be shown combined.
  • Unpublished drafts are kept in your browser. If you choose "Save draft to your account", the draft goes to a secret gist (GitHub) or to the app's hidden folder in your Drive (Google), which only you can access.

Published portfolios are public: anyone with the link can view them. Don't publish anything you want to keep private.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, mz-portfolio asks for two permissions and nothing else:

  • drive.file: to create, read, update and delete only the files and folder this app creates in your Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • drive.appdata: to keep your private draft in the app's hidden Drive folder.

It also reads your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account, only to show who is signed in. The app creates the "mz-portfolio" folder and shares it as "anyone with the link can view", because that is what makes the portfolio public.

mz-portfolio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features you use on this site. It is never sold, never used for advertising, never transferred to anyone else, and never read by people.

Sign-in tokens

Signing in gives your browser an access token. It is kept in your browser's local storage until you sign out or it expires (Google tokens last about an hour), and it is only sent to GitHub's or Google's own APIs. Signing in with GitHub uses a small server function on this site to complete the login; it doesn't keep any record.

Analytics and cookies

mz-portfolio has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. During GitHub sign-in a short-lived cookie protects the login from forgery and is deleted right after.

Removing your data

  • Sign out from the account menu to remove the token from your browser.
  • Delete the gists from GitHub, or the "mz-portfolio" folder from your Google Drive, to delete your portfolio.
  • Revoke access any time in your Google account permissions or GitHub authorized apps.
